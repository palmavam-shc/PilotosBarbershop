Batangas Harbor Pilot Capt. Harold Janda

As a boy, I dreamed of becoming a pilot, though my family’s simple living made flying school out of reach. Instead, watching my father work as a ferry skipper on the Batangas–Mindoro route inspired me to become a ship captain. I graduated from the Lyceum International Maritime Academy (LIMA) in 1991 and spent over two decades at sea, eventually fulfilling my dream of becoming a Captain. Seeking a new challenge in 2014, I set my sights on becoming a Harbor Pilot to serve my hometown at the Port of Batangas. After completing the required five years as a Master Mariner—even spending my vacations gaining experience on Montenegro Shipping Lines vessels—I passed the licensure exam and started in Bacolod as LIMA’s first Harbor Pilot alumnus. Four years later, I finally joined the prestigious Batangas Harbor Pilot Co. As I shared at LPU-Batangas’ 52nd Commencement Exercise, achieving your dreams means becoming your own PILOT: Pray with all your heart, stay Inspired, Live without fear, remain Optimistic, and manage your Time well. Oh—and I eventually fulfilled my childhood dream of flying a plane, too! “Fly High on High Seas!” #ButiNaLangNagLyceumAko!
